SILTA

CNC planning before production

For mechanical designers and CAM programmers
in CNC shops.

The product

Agents turn a drawing into CAD and a machining plan, then simulate and revise it in Fusion.

Saved checks and instructions help avoid repeating mistakes on future parts.

The business case

Less repetitive programming. More time for design and manufacturing decisions.

Check feasibility and estimate machining time with the shop’s equipment before quoting or cutting material.

People review and approve the plan before production.
