- Submit on the AGI House platform. One teammate submits; list every member.
- Every teammate must sign in and complete the participant survey before submission can complete.
- Include a unique team name, project name, a 2–3 sentence summary, team roster, socials, repository URL, track, and sponsor tool/protocol descriptions.
- Explain what the agent does and how feedback improves its next attempt. Document frameworks, orchestration protocols, and RL environments actually used.
