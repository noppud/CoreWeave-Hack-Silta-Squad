## Pitch priorities (our recommendation)

Lead with one concrete failed CAM attempt, the actual feedback, its accepted repair
and the verified result. Then show the reusable check or prompt change and its
measured evaluation. Use same-part before/after machining times; don't imply that
cross-part time differences prove learning.

For Weave, show a real run, finalized paired evaluation and actual custom monitoring
feedback. For ARIA, show the real review and implemented focus-readiness fix,
separating the independent OCR correction from ARIA's contribution. Describe the
marimo workbench as the actual evidence interface. Avoid claiming native Signals,
weight training, physical-machine validation or complete general five-axis check
coverage. End with the useful outcome and the next concrete verification boundary.
