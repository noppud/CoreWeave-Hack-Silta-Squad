# Submission copy — draft, not submitted

SILTA keeps a customer's part fixed while an Astra CAM agent proposes faster manufacturing plans. Deterministic checks and swept-tool stock-removal simulation reject incorrect plans; a timing-only judge requests further optimization, and saved failure memory catches exact repeated mistakes before another full simulation.

The demo includes three new complex indexed parts with up to 26 features and 17 orientations, a live reviewed-PDF workflow, downloadable movements and stock meshes, and inspectable prompts/checks/memory. Across the three new passing reference/best pairs, estimated machining time fell by 72.5%. Within the feedback loop, the carrier improved a further 6.4%; the manifold tied, while a slower drum proposal was retained as evidence and its earlier best stayed selected. Selected fresh counterexamples show bounded repeat-failure avoidance; a 24-plan transfer study across four parts did not establish a general memory-driven speed benefit.
